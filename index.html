Professor of Mathematics

Taylan Şengül

Department of Mathematics · Marmara University

I am an applied mathematician working on nonlinear dynamical systems and partial differential equations. My research focuses on dynamic transitions, bifurcation and pattern formation, with applications to classical and geophysical fluid dynamics.

Email CV Google Scholar AVESIS ResearchGate

Research

My work combines qualitative methods, reductions and stability analysis to understand how nonlinear systems change regime and form coherent structures.

Dynamical systems and pattern formation

Dynamic transitions, bifurcations, symmetry, model reduction and patterns in nonlinear evolution equations.

Fluid dynamics and instability

Classical and geophysical fluid dynamics, hydrodynamic instabilities and transitions in atmosphere-ocean models.

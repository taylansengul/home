Professor of Mathematics

Taylan Şengül

Department of Mathematics · Marmara University

I am an applied mathematician working on nonlinear dynamical systems and partial differential equations. My research focuses on dynamic transitions, bifurcation and pattern formation, with applications to classical and geophysical fluid dynamics.

Email CV Google Scholar AVESIS

Research

  • Transition numbers in closed form · explicit formulas for the transition coefficient of 1D reaction–diffusion equations.
  • Symmetry and pattern selection · what degenerate transitions select; currently Hopf bifurcation under O(2) symmetry.
  • Fluids · the same machinery applied to the thermohaline circulation, zonal ocean flows and Poiseuille flow.
